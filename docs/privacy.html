WildLens

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Applies to wildlens.in and the WildLens Android app

Overview

WildLens is a free, non-commercial platform that maps Indian wildlife and environment news. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. There is no account system, no login, and no tracking.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No name, email, phone, or contact information
  • No location data from your device
  • No usage analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No cookies set by WildLens
  • No advertising identifiers

News content

WildLens aggregates publicly available news articles from third-party RSS feeds. We display headlines, publication dates, source names, and article links. We do not store article content — only metadata needed to display the map pin (headline, URL, source, date, location coordinates).

Clicking "Read article" opens the original publisher's website in your browser. That publisher's own privacy policy applies to your visit there.

Third-party services

  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap — provides the map tiles. Your IP address may be visible to their servers when tiles are loaded. See CARTO Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — loads the Inter typeface. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. See GitHub Privacy Policy.

Offline caching

The WildLens app and website use a service worker to cache files on your device for offline access. This cache contains only app files and the latest news data — no personal information. You can clear this cache at any time through your browser or app settings.

Children

WildLens does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no social features.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of WildLens constitutes acceptance of any changes.
