WildLens

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to wildlens.in and the WildLens Android app

Overview

WildLens is a free, non-commercial platform that maps Indian wildlife and environment news. We have no account system and no login, and we do not sell any data. To understand how many people use the site, we use privacy-respecting analytics (described below) — one of which sets cookies and only runs if you consent.

Data we collect

We collect no personally identifying data. Specifically:

  • No name, email, phone, or contact information
  • No precise location data from your device
  • No advertising identifiers, and we do not sell any data

Analytics & cookies

We use two analytics tools purely to measure aggregate traffic (visits, page views, approximate region, referrer). We never use them to identify you personally.

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — cookieless and privacy-first. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data, so it runs for all visitors. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — sets cookies, so it loads only if you click "Accept" on the consent banner. If you decline (or ignore it), Google Analytics never loads and no analytics cookies are set. IP addresses are anonymised. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser. See Google Privacy Policy.

News content

WildLens aggregates publicly available news articles from third-party RSS feeds. We display headlines, publication dates, source names, and article links. We do not store article content — only metadata needed to display the map pin (headline, URL, source, date, location coordinates).

Clicking "Read article" opens the original publisher's website in your browser. That publisher's own privacy policy applies to your visit there.

Third-party services

  • Esri / OpenStreetMap — provide the map tiles. Your IP address may be visible to their servers when tiles are loaded. See Esri Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — loads the Inter typeface. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. See GitHub Privacy Policy.

Offline caching

The WildLens app and website use a service worker to cache files on your device for offline access. This cache contains only app files and the latest news data — no personal information. You can clear this cache at any time through your browser or app settings.

Children

WildLens does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no social features.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of WildLens constitutes acceptance of any changes.
