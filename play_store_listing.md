## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
WildLens puts India's wildlife and environmental news on a single interactive map. Instead of scrolling through dozens of news sites, see where stories are happening — tiger sightings, elephant corridors, poaching busts, forest fires, human-wildlife conflict, and conservation wins — as pins across the country.

Tap any pin to read the headline, source, and date, then open the full article on the original publisher's site.

WHAT MAKES WILDLENS DIFFERENT

• One map, every region — News is pulled from national outlets and dozens of regional and local publications, so stories that never reach the English national press still show up.

• True multilingual coverage — WildLens tracks wildlife news in Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Odia, and Assamese, with headlines translated to English. A separate Regional edition lets you browse by language.

• Filter by what matters — Narrow the map by category (poaching & crime, human-wildlife conflict, species discovery, research, conservation & policy), by news source, by date range, or by search term.

• See the last 60 days at a glance — The map shows recent news by default, with a one-tap option to explore older stories going further back.

• Completely free — No ads. No subscriptions. No account or sign-up. No tracking. WildLens collects no personal data whatsoever.

WHO IT'S FOR

Wildlife enthusiasts, conservationists, researchers, students, journalists, forest department staff, and anyone who cares about India's natural heritage and wants a faster, clearer way to follow environmental news across the country.

HOW IT WORKS

WildLens continuously gathers wildlife and environment news from public news feeds, identifies the most specific Indian location in each story, and places it on the map. Everything runs on free, open infrastructure — there is no company harvesting your data behind the scenes.

Note: WildLens aggregates publicly available news and links to original articles. It is an independent, non-commercial project and is not affiliated with any government body or news publisher.

Have feedback or found a bug? Email nikunj@wildlens.in — we'd love to hear from you.
```
